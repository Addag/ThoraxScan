# Model manifest and configuration

`ModelManifestV1` is the replaceable-model boundary containing the ONNX artifact contract, ordered labels and thresholds, preprocessing, postprocessing, provenance, and parity evidence hashes.

## Why this shape

Weights and thresholds can be replaced without an application deployment only while the fixed version 1 parser accepts the bundle.

## Shape

Version 1 fixes a single-file opset-17 artifact, float32 `[batch,1,224,224]` input, 14 logits, grayscale normalization, the 14-label order, inclusive thresholds, `No Finding` suppression, and explicit no-match behavior.

## Connected to

Describes a [Model release](model-release.md), drives the [Preprocessing tensor](preprocessing-tensor.md), and interprets the [Transient prediction result](prediction-result.md).

## If you change this

### Hits

- Validator, CLI bundle evidence, browser runtime, preprocessing, postprocessing, parity tests, and registry manifest checks.

### Does not hit

- Training weights or internal architecture when their exported artifact still satisfies version 1.

## Surfaces

Generated by the operator validator, stored by Supabase, and parsed again by the browser before model loading.
